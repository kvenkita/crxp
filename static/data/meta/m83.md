## Job density
Workplace jobs per square mile (LEHD LODES WAC over tract land area)

### Why is this important?
Job density marks employment centers and the commercial/industrial intensity that drives traffic, energy use, and night-time light. Higher and lower values are not inherently better or worse.

### About the Data
Workplace jobs from the Census Bureau's LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES8 Workplace Area Characteristics, 2014–2022), summed from 2020 census blocks to tracts. LODES is a synthetic, noise-infused full count rather than a survey sample, so it carries no sampling margin of error.

_**Source**: U.S. Census Bureau, LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES)._
